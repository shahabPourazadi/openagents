# Synthesize skill

1. `read_document` and identify themes vs one-off facts.
2. Propose a short synthesis paragraph under Findings (or a new **Synthesis** subsection).
3. Call out disagreements between sources.
4. End with 2–4 concrete next steps the user can act on.
